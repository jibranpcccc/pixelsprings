07. THE FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have a specific framework or workload in mind? We are glad to talk through your agency's operating model.

Speak with our partners →

What does unlimited requests mean?

+
You can add as many design, web, and development requests to your queue as you like. They will be completed systematically, one after another (or concurrently depending on your active plan tier).

How many requests can I have active?

+
Pixel Spark includes 1 active request at a time. Pixel Flow includes 2 active requests concurrently, and Pixel Forge handles 3 active requests in parallel.

How long does a request take?

+
Most standard design tasks and component layouts are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Complex web applications, full redesigns, and custom backend modules are broken down into transparent sprint milestones.

What happens with revisions?

+
Revisions are 100% unlimited. We iterate on the deliverables until you and your clients are completely satisfied.

Can I pause or cancel?

+
Yes, our billing cycles are 31 days. If you only have tasks for 14 days, you can pause your cycle and preserve the remaining 17 days to use anytime in the future.

Do you work with agencies & is it white label?

+
Over 60% of our production bandwidth is dedicated to partner agencies. We operate completely behind the scenes under strict NDA agreements with zero Pixelsprings branding.

Can you handle large websites?

+
Yes. Large websites with custom CMS architectures, e-commerce, and APIs are planned as progressive page sprints, keeping you fully in control of the rollout timeline.

# PixelSprings — Production Website Showcase

Two complete, full-page HTML websites built from high-fidelity screencapture blueprints:

1. **Design 1: Editorial Studio** (`design1-editorial/`)
   - High-end Swiss-grid magazine aesthetic on ivory cream (`#FAF9F6`).
   - Editorial typography (`Instrument Serif` + `Plus Jakarta Sans`).
   - 5 high-resolution photography case studies.
   - White-label agency partnership workflow matrix.
   - 3-tier pricing ($799 - $2,499).
   - Interactive FAQ accordion & inquiry lead capture form.

2. **Design 2: Vibrant Tech Agency** (`design2-vibrant/`)
   - Dark obsidian hero (`#0B0D11`) with electric neon lime accents (`#84CC16`).
   - 3D device mockup graphics & delivery stack badges.
   - 8-card portfolio showcase grid.
   - Founders note from Noman & Umair.
   - 3 dark glassmorphic subscription cards.
   - Testimonial from Salman Khan (CEO at Being Human).
   - 14-item interactive FAQ accordion & human check lead form.
